## Telegram post template<a name="telegram-post-template"></a>

```
[HOOK]
- Rhetorical question or shocking fact to grab attention.

[INTRO]
- Short, friendly opening on the topic.
- Think about it: ask the reader a quick question or share a personal comment.

[THESIS]
Clear statement you're going to prove.
(1 paragraph, 2-3 sentences)

[EVIDENCE]
Numbers, examples, and personal observations:

- Comparisons: 10% vs 20%.
- Hypothetical stories: "If you only used deposits...".
- Light irony: "Or you wait for a discount on pasta...".

[PERSONAL INSIGHT]
A short personal example or mini-story:
"Here's how my portfolio looks right now..."

[CONCLUSION]
Clear summary: restate the main point in simple terms.

[ACTIONABLE ADVICE]
One specific tip:
"Start by checking your actual allocation today."

[CTA]
Light call-to-action:
"Subscribe for more investing tips"
"Hit like if you found this helpful!"
```

## Narrative Style<a name="narrative-style"></a>

- Conversational yet Expert: The tone is friendly but backed by authority. The language feels approachable - no heavy jargon, but occasional professional terms keep it credible.
- Trust-building tone: Frequent use of "think about it", "in your case", "I'll try to convince you" - creates a personal dialogue with the reader.
- Rhetorical questions: Engages readers early - ("What's your actual allocation to stocks right now?").
- Step-by-step breakdown: Clear logical flow - problem -> data -> conclusion -> advice.
- Illustrative examples: Real-world cases and hypotheticals ("if you saved 10%", "if you only used deposits").

## Techniques and Devices<a name="techniques-and-devices"></a>

- Light humor and irony: Phrases like "wait for a discount on pasta" - it softens the seriousness, makes the text more human.
- Strong use of numbers and comparisons: 10%, 16%, 20% - lots of specific figures to support arguments.
- Personal anecdotes: Mentions about personal accounts, cash holdings - builds credibility and relatability.
- Focus on reader benefit: Emphasizes what the reader gains ("save more with less effort", "build a bigger pension").

## Pacing<a name="pacing"></a>

- Moderate, deliberate: The material unfolds slowly and thoughtfully, without overwhelming.
- Momentum spikes: Pacing speeds up briefly with strings of examples or strong conclusions ("think about it...", "the difference is huge").

## Structure<a name="structure"></a>

- Question -> Thesis -> Evidence -> Conclusion: Ideal for short, punchy posts.
- Frequent focus shifts: From general logic to personal examples and back.
- Calls-to-action (CTA): "Like", "Subscribe" - gently placed, non-intrusive.

## Emotion and Tone<a name="emotion-and-tone"></a>

- Mild urgency: Subtle fear of missed opportunities ("keeping everything in deposits").
- Motivational: Emphasizes the long-term benefits of sticking with stocks.

## Audience<a name="audience"></a>

- Beginners to intermediate investors: Accessible explanations without being patronizing.
- Enthusiastic, non-professionals: People curious about growing their wealth and planning for retirement.

## Adaptations for Telegram Posts<a name="adaptations-for-telegram-posts"></a>

- Short paragraphs (3-5 lines): Mobile-first readability.
- Hooks at the top: Start with a question or a shocking stat.
- Highlight key figures: Use bold or emojis.
- Soft CTA at the end: "Subscribe for more", "Hit like if useful" - keep it light.
- First-person insights: Share personal experience to build trust ("Here's how my portfolio looks...").

## Ideal Post Formula Based on This Style<a name="ideal-post-formula-based-on-this-style"></a>

- Start with a hook: A question or a surprising fact.
- 2-3 short paragraphs of explanation: Keep language simple.
- Hard numbers and comparisons: Tables, stats, examples.
- A small relatable story: Personal or hypothetical.
- Conclusion + 1 piece of actionable advice.
- Gentle call-to-action.
